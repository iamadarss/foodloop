Is System Ke 3 Pramukh Stambh

FoodLoop me teen tarah ke sadasya milkar food waste ko khatam karte hain:

👨‍🍳
Stambh 1

1. Food Donors (Bhojan Daata)

Hotels, restaurants, bakehouses, catering companies ya shaadi-party ke aayojak jinke paas taza khana bach gaya ho.

  • Form me khana ka naam, quantity aur category bharein.
  • Pickup location aur expiry deadline set karein.
  • Khana fenkne ke bajay kisi ki thali me pahuchayein.
🚴
Stambh 2

2. Volunteers & Couriers (Swayamsevak)

Yuva, college students, aur local nagrik jo donor se khana lekar shelter ya basti tak surakshit pahuchate hain.

  • Browse section me nearby khana dekhein aur claim karein.
  • Secure PIN (FL-XXXX) lekar donor ke paas jayein.
  • 1-click status update: "Picked Up" → "Delivered".
🏠
Stambh 3

3. Beneficiary Shelters (Ashray & NGO)

Old-age homes, orphanages, rain basera, aur community kitchens jahan bhookhe parivaron ko taza poshtik bhojan milta hai.

  • Nirantar fresh meal supply bina kisi kharch ke.
  • Surakshit hygiene aur temperature-checked items.
  • Community impact matrix me har thali ka record.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Khana List Karne Se Deliver Hone Tak

Puri prakriya 5 aasan steps me poori hoti hai:

01
Kadam 1: Bhojan Suchibaddh Karein

Add Food: Surplus Food Ka Vivaran Dalein

Jab restaurant ya kitchen me bacha hua taza khana ho, toh "+ Donate Food" button par click karein. Yahan item ka naam (e.g. 20 Fresh Roti & Dal Containers), quantity, pickup address aur pickup deadline dalein. Aap photo presets (Bakery, Produce, Prepared Meals) chun sakte hain aur live preview dekh sakte hain.

Try Step 1: Add Food Form →
02
Kadam 2: Filter & Search Karein

Browse Food: Available Khana Khojein

Volunteers aur shelters "Browse Food" section me real-time catalog dekhte hain. Aap Category (Bakery, Vegetables, Prepared Meals), Dietary Preference (Vegetarian, Vegan), ya Expiry Deadline ke mutabik search kar sakte hain.

Try Step 2: Browse Food Catalog →
03
Kadam 3: Reservation & Verification PIN

Reserve Food: Item Ko Secure Karein

Kisi available item par "Reserve Food" dabane par ek pop-up khulta hai. Yahan volunteer apna naam aur charity organization bharte hain. System turant ek Verification PIN (e.g. FL-6821) issue karta hai. Duplicate reservation prevention engine ye ensure karta hai ki ek item ko ek hi samay do log na book kar lein.

04
Kadam 4: Pickup & Stepper Progression

Pickup: Donor Se Khana Collect Karein

Volunteer pickup location par pahuch kar PIN dikhata hai. Phir app me "Confirm Food Picked Up" button dabata hai. Status turant Reserved se badalkar Picked Up ho jata hai aur pipeline stepper agle charn par chala jata hai.

05
Kadam 5: Delivery & Live Impact

Mark Delivered: Thali Tak Khana Pahucha Kar Record Karein

Shelter me khana deliver hone par "Mark as Delivered" par click karein. Ye donation Completed ho jata hai aur turant Dashboard par Total Rescued Food (kg), Meals Provided, aur CO2 Emissions Saved dynamically update ho jate hain!

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

❓ Kya FoodLoop ka upyog karne ke liye koi charges lagte hain? ▼
Nahi! FoodLoop 100% free community-driven platform hai. Na donor se paise liye jate hain aur na hi shelters se.
❓ Kis tarah ka khana donate kiya ja sakta hai? ▼
Fresh bakery items, unbroken bread, organic taze fal-sabziyan, commercial kitchens ke prepared hygienically pack meals, aur packaged groceries jinki shelf life bachi ho. Khana kharab, basi ya khula nahi hona chahiye.
❓ Kya data internet na hone par bhi save rehta hai? ▼
Haan! FoodLoop aapke browser aur Android APK me HTML5 LocalStorage API use karta hai. Sabhi demo listings, reservations, aur dashboard statistics bina kisi external database ke phone me turant save ho jate hain.
❓ Agar demo data kharab ho jaye to kya reset kar sakte hain? ▼
Bilkul! Top banner me aur Dashboard par "Reset Demo Data" button diya gaya hai, jisse ek click me shuruati sample listings wapas aa jati hain.
