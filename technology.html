3. Intelligent Algorithms & Calculations

Calculations driving nutritional and environmental metrics in FoodLoop.

Duplicate Prevention Engine

Prevents multiple charities or couriers from claiming the same perishable stock. The claim validator verifies both listing availability and reservation queue status before committing atomic updates to storage.

Carbon Footprint Avoidance Model

Computes ecological savings utilizing verified UN FAO benchmarks: Every 1 kg of edible organic food diverted from municipal landfills prevents approximately 1.9 kg CO2e in atmospheric methane emissions.

📊

4. Zero-Dependency SVG Visualizations

Lightweight, responsive charts rendered directly via mathematical SVG generation.

Instead of importing megabyte-heavy charting libraries (e.g. Chart.js, Highcharts), FoodLoop utilizes mathematical curve calculations (cubic Béziers) and circle stroke-dash geometry with Tiranga color mapping at under 3 KB total script size:

  • Trend Chart: Continuous cubic Bézier control points (C cpX1 cpY1, cpX2 cpY2, x y) with saffron-to-green gradient fills and interactive data point badges.
  • Category Donut Chart: Dynamic stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset mapped to category percentages with centered live tally.
  • Reactive Re-rendering: Whenever a new donation is marked delivered, the charts automatically re-plot with new metrics.
